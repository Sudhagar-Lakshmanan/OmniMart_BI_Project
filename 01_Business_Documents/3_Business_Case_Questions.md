# Business Case Questions & Insights

*(Extracted from Business Requirements Document — BRD)* 

These questions are designed to guide analysis, align reporting with business objectives, and provide actionable insights.

---

## 1. Revenue

The following questions focus on understanding revenue trends, growth, and key revenue drivers.

| Question No. | Business Question                       | Metric / Focus Area                | Purpose / Insight                                                             |
|--------------|------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Q1           | Revenue Summary Metrics                 | Gross Revenue, Net Revenue, Discounts | Assess true earning impact before and after discounts                        |
| Q2           | Year-over-Year Revenue Growth           | Monthly Revenue Trends              | Identify seasonal momentum and long-term growth                               |
| Q3           | Revenue by Product Categories           | Category-Level Revenue              | Prioritize high-performing categories for inventory & marketing              |
| Q4           | Top 10 Revenue-Generating Products      | Product-Level Revenue               | Spotlight best-selling SKUs driving commercial performance                   |
| Q5           | Revenue by Payment Method               | Payment Choices                     | Understand revenue influence based on payment behaviour                       |
| Q6           | State-Wise Revenue Performance          | Regional Revenue Comparison         | Evaluate geographic strengths and opportunity zones                           |
